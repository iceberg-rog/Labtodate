# Homepage 3D assets

- `globe/night_*`, `globe/region_night.webp`: NASA Black Marble 2016 (public domain), graded for the route globe.
- `globe/lights_*`, `globe/region_lights.webp`, `globe/pack_1k.jpg` (bump / land mask / clouds): Solar System Scope textures, CC BY 4.0 (https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/).
- `globe/day_*`: NASA Blue Marble Next Generation, July (public domain).
- `globe/atlas_*`, `globe/borders-50m.json`: derived from Natural Earth 1:50m (public domain) and world-atlas (ISC).
- `env/studio_env_512.webp`: Poly Haven "Ferndale Studio 12" HDRI (CC0), graded and baked to 8-bit.

Prepared for the Shelter landing (same owner) and reused here unchanged.
